AutoVerse – AI Car Price Predictor & Showroom

A Streamlit web app that predicts the fair market price of a used car with a machine-learning model, shows the car in a 4K showroom, and lets you buy it with a full checkout flow.

✨ Features
AI price prediction – Polynomial-feature Linear Regression (R² ≈ 0.92) trained on used-car data; live estimate with a fair price range.
33 car models, 11 brands – BMW, Mercedes, Skoda, Maruti, Honda, Hyundai, Kia, Mahindra, Tata, Toyota, Ford. Specs auto-fill per model.
4K car images – photos fetched from Wikimedia Commons at up to 3840 px wide, with an animated illustration as fallback.
Buy a car – checkout form, full payment or car loan with EMI calculator, order receipt and a "My Garage" of purchased cars.
Animated UI – glassmorphism, animated gradient background, floating orbs, Ken Burns image effect and a count-up price.
